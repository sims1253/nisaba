Proposal B — Ink Desk

or, the interface is the ink; the document is the light ↩ current design record

Ink Desk inverts Nisaba's material: every piece of apparatus — bars, navigator, docks, drawer, status — becomes one warm bitumen, and the two surfaces that are the document, the source column and the rendered page, become the only paper, so they glow for it. The accent becomes lapis, the scribe's prestige material; the serif is promoted from the rendered page into the chrome's few big moments; and the projects screen becomes a table of contents — your work presented the way a document presents itself. Same workflow, same bones: prose-first, docks-not-modals, palette-driven, collaboration always visible.
