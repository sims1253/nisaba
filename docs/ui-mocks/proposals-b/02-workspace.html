Honey Badgers at Fairy Raves: An Interspecies Acoustic Analysis

twinkleburst · sparkletoes · clawson — Field lab

Abstract

We document seventeen months of field observations of honey badgers (Mellivora capensis) attending subterranean fairy raves in the Welsh countryside. Using enchanted monitoring crystals and glitter-spectroscopy, we characterise attendance patterns, acoustic entrainment, and interspecies social dynamics. Our findings suggest a stable co-occurrence relationship mediated by low-frequency bass.

Honey badgers attended 74% of recorded rave events (n = 57). Vocalisations in the 40–60 Hz range were phase-locked to the fairy audio system (Table 1).
