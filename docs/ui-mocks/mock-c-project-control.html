= Honey Badgers at Fairy Raves: An Interspecies Acoustic Analysis
 
== Abstract
We document seventeen months of field observations of honey badgers (Mellivora capensis) attending subterranean fairy raves in the Welsh countryside. Using enchanted monitoring crystals and glitter-spectroscopy, we characterize attendance patterns, acoustic entrainment, and interspecies social dynamics. Our findings suggest a stable co-occurrence relationship mediated by low-frequency bass.
 
== Introduction
The honey badger has long been regarded as a solitary species. Recent anecdotal reports, however, describe badgers congregating at fairy social gatherings #cite(<twinkleburst-2024>), raising questions about the ecological drivers of this behaviour. Some researchers dispute this entirely.
 
Field sites were selected across Cwm Derwen and Tywyn Hollow. Monitoring stations recorded continuously for 510 days. Acoustic analysis used cross-correlation of vocalization envelopes with the dominant bass frequency.
 
== Results
Honey badgers attended 74% of recorded rave events. Vocalizations in the 40–60 Hz range were phase-locked to the fairy audio system.
