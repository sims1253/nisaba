== Abstract
We document seventeen months of field observations of honey
badgers (_Mellivora capensis_) attending subterranean fairy
raves in the Welsh countryside. Using enchanted monitoring
crystals and glitter-spectroscopy, we characterize
attendance patterns, acoustic entrainment, and interspecies

== Background
The honey badger has long been regarded as a solitary species.
Recent anecdotal reports, however, describe badgers
congregating at fairy social gatherings
#cite(<twinkleburst-2024>), raising questions
about the ecological drivers of this behaviour. Some
researchers dispute this entirely.

TWINKLEBURST ET AL.FIELD LAB

Honey Badgers at Fairy Raves: An Interspecies Acoustic Analysis

We document seventeen months of field observations of honey badgers (Mellivora capensis) attending subterranean fairy raves in the Welsh countryside. Using enchanted monitoring crystals and glitter-spectroscopy, we characterize attendance patterns, acoustic entrainment, and interspecies social dynamics.

Honey badgers attended 74% of recorded rave events. Vocalizations in the 40–60 Hz range were phase-locked to the fairy audio system.
